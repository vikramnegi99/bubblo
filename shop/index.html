FAQ

Shopping questions

What payment method is available?
BUBBLO currently accepts Cash On Delivery (COD) only. You pay in cash when your order is delivered. No online payment, card, UPI, wallet or net-banking is collected on this website.
Is Cash On Delivery available?
Yes. Every order is placed on Cash On Delivery. Once you place an order, we contact you to confirm it before it is dispatched.
Where does BUBBLO deliver?
We deliver within India, wherever our delivery partners can service. Share your pincode with us on WhatsApp and we'll confirm serviceability for your area.
How do I choose the Pink or Blue variant?
On the LED Lotus Flower Bubble Wand page, use the colour selector to switch between Pink (₹999) and Blue (₹899). The images, price, MRP and discount update instantly. Both are variants of the same product — not separate products.
How does the selected variant appear in my order?
The variant you select follows you everywhere: the product page, the cart, the checkout, your order summary and the order success page. If you add both Pink and Blue, they appear as two separate lines with their own prices and images.
